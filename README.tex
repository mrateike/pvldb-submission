% Online Appendix

This repository contains the online appendix for our VLDB submission. It provides supplementary material that accompanies the paper, including extended experimental results, model details, and additional analyses that could not be included in the main paper due to space constraints.

\section*{Contents}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Appendix A: Model Details} --- Additional information on the prediction models used in our evaluation.
    \item \textbf{Appendix B: Baselines} --- Additional information on the baseline methods used in our evaluation.
    \item \textbf{Appendix C: Extended Per Query Results} --- Extended per query analyses for TPC-DS and StormSQL benchmarks.
    \item \textbf{Appendix D: Hardware and Setup} --- Additional information on hardware and setup.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Additional Artifacts}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Results Data} --- Data used to generate the figures in the main paper and appendix (available in the \texttt{data} directory).
    \item \textbf{Models} --- Trained prediction models used to generate results in the paper and appendix (available in the \texttt{models} directory).
\end{itemize}
